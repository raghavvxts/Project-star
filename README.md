# Project Star 🌟

This is a deeply personal project, built with every bit of thought, care, and emotion I have. It was created to celebrate a special birthday for someone who means everything to me—Vedanshi.

I poured my heart into every line of code, every animation, and every image, hoping to create a small, memorable experience that could convey what words sometimes fail to say. It was meant to be a reminder of how much she is loved, a testament to the fact that she is worth every effort, and a reflection of the warmth she brings to my life.

Life doesn't always go as planned, and paths can diverge. But the intention behind this project remains the same. 

This is my heartfelt, last birthday message to you, Vedanshi. Maybe someday you will stumble upon this repository and read this. I want you to know that building this was my way of showing you how much you truly mean to me. Even if circumstances have changed, the love and effort poured into this remain true.

I hope wherever you are, and whenever you read this, that you are happy, smiling, and looking up at the stars.

Happy Birthday.
